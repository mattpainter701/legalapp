# Account safety & help

Your account represents your identity in approvals, edits, time, and audit history. Keep it personal and protect it like any other system containing client information.

## Everyday safety

- Use your own account and approved sign-in method.
- Lock your device when stepping away.
- Sign out on shared or temporary devices.
- Confirm the tenant, matter, and recipients before working with sensitive content.
- Do not paste passwords, tokens, recovery codes, or unrelated client data into the assistant.

If you suspect unauthorized access, sign out and contact your administrator immediately. Include the approximate time and what you observed, but do not send credentials through chat or email.

## Understand your profile

[Your profile](/profile) shows your account identity, role, license context, assigned matters, and recent time totals. Role and license changes are managed by an administrator. Module availability may also depend on your firm's plan.

## Get useful help

When reporting a problem, include:

1. the page and action you were using;
2. what you expected to happen;
3. what happened instead;
4. the time of the problem and whether it repeats; and
5. any request or error ID shown by the interface.

Use redacted screenshots when they make the issue clearer. Avoid copying full client documents into a support message unless your approved support process specifically requests them.

For product access, assignments, integration status, or firm policy, contact your LawHand administrator first. For a suspected security or privacy incident, follow your firm's incident process without delay.
