# Time, billing & reports

Financial features depend on your role and the modules your firm has enabled. If a destination below is unavailable, ask an administrator which workflow your firm uses.

## Record time

Open [Time Tracking](/time-tracking) and enter time close to when the work occurred. Select the correct matter, date, duration, activity, and billing status. A concise narrative should tell a reviewer what professional work was performed without including unnecessary sensitive detail.

Check for duplicate timers or entries before saving. If you need to correct posted or approved time, follow firm policy rather than offsetting it with an unexplained entry.

## Invoices

Open [Invoices](/invoices) to review invoices available to you. Confirm the client, matter, time and expense lines, adjustments, dates, and payment status. Treat generated descriptions and totals as reviewable work product until the responsible person approves them.

Invoice PDFs use the tenant's configured firm name, logo, address, contact
details, and optional PDF footer. The PDF shows the matter name and current
balance but does not expose internal tenant or matter identifiers. Ask an
administrator to correct firm branding before sending a bill rather than
editing each exported copy.

## Trust accounting

Use [Trust Accounting](/trust) only if you are authorized and trained for your jurisdiction and firm. Choose the correct client ledger before recording a transaction. Preserve the source documentation and never use a balancing entry to conceal an unexplained difference.

Trust rules vary. The software helps maintain records; it does not decide whether a transfer is ethically or legally permitted.

## Reports

[Reports](/reports) summarizes operational and financial data such as matters, intake, overdue tasks, work in progress, realization, and aging. A report reflects the records entered and the filters selected. Check the date range and scope before relying on a figure or exporting it.

If a number looks wrong, trace it back to the underlying matter, time entry, invoice, or payment rather than editing a downstream summary.
