# Domestic relations workflows

The [Domestic Relations portfolio](/plugins/domestic/cases) provides structured tracking for family-law cases. Use it with the controlling matter, pleadings, orders, financial evidence, and jurisdiction-specific procedures.

## Case overview

Confirm the case name, parties, court, case number, jurisdiction, assigned professionals, status, and key dates. Avoid casual labels for parties or children; use verified names and the roles used by the proceeding.

## Detail tabs

- **Parties** records participant identity and roles.
- **Children** records the children relevant to the case.
- **Custody** describes current or proposed arrangements.
- **Support Calculator** prepares a reviewable calculation from entered facts.
- **Orders & Payments** tracks support orders and payment activity.
- **Deadlines** tracks procedural and court dates.
- **Saved Calcs** preserves calculation versions for later comparison.

## Calculations

Verify income periods, adjustments, parenting time, insurance, childcare, dependents, deviations, and jurisdictional inputs. Save a new calculation when material facts change rather than overwriting the context of an earlier analysis.

A calculated amount is not a court order and may not reflect every statutory or factual issue. Label drafts and obtain professional review.

## Sensitive information

Domestic cases may contain addresses, financial records, health details, abuse allegations, and information about children. Follow sealing, redaction, protected-address, and access requirements. Do not expose restricted information through a general note, portal invitation, export, or assistant prompt.
