# Add-on module management

[Add-on Modules](/plugins) is the catalog for specialized LawHand workflows. Visibility and availability depend on the tenant plan, entitlement, setup state, role, and module configuration.

## Module states

- **Purchased** modules are entitled and available when setup and access conditions are satisfied.
- **Trials** are time- or scope-limited evaluations.
- **Setup Required** modules need configuration or a completed practice profile.
- **Available** modules may be eligible for purchase or enablement.
- **Locked** modules are unavailable under the current plan, entitlement, or administrative policy.

Do not interpret a visible catalog card as permission to use a module for client work. Confirm entitlement, setup, firm procedure, and your role.

## Complete module setup

Open a module to review its setup workflow. Some modules conduct a structured interview and produce a firm or practice profile. Answer with approved organization-wide information, review the generated profile, and correct unsupported assumptions before marking setup complete.

A practice profile may influence later AI outputs across the tenant. Do not insert matter-specific secrets or one lawyer's personal preferences into a shared profile unless that is the intended and approved scope.

## Run module analysis

Specialized modules may accept matter facts or documents and produce structured analysis. Choose the right matter and source, follow required gates, inspect warnings, and validate conclusions against the governing law and record.

Preserve material source documents and final work product in the matter. A module result shown on screen is not automatically part of the official client file.

## When setup or analysis fails

Read the displayed gate or error. Common causes include an incomplete practice profile, missing source document, unsupported file type, unavailable entitlement, or disconnected integration. Avoid repeated submissions when the failure could duplicate work or incur usage.

Contact an administrator for entitlement, tenant profile, integration, or configuration issues. Include the module, step, time, matter identifier, and any request ID without sending unnecessary client content.
