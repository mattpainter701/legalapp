# Start here

LawHand brings matters, tasks, communications, time, documents, and approved AI tools into one authenticated workspace. What you see depends on the modules your firm has enabled and the work assigned to you.

## Your first five minutes

1. Open [My Matters](/matters) and confirm that your current files appear.
2. Open [Tasks](/tasks) and check work that is due or overdue.
3. Review [Calendar](/calendar) for deadlines and scheduled events.
4. Visit [your profile](/profile) to confirm your name, role, license, and personal activity.
5. Bookmark this guide. You can return from the guide card on your profile.

## Find your way around

The left navigation groups everyday work by purpose. **Workspace** holds shared operational tools. **Accounting** contains time, invoices, trust, and reporting features when your role and firm plan allow them. **Add-on Modules** contains specialized practice workflows purchased by your firm.

On a smaller screen, open the navigation with the menu button. Choosing a destination closes the menu so the selected page has the full screen.

## Access is intentional

Your account only shows enabled modules and permitted records. A missing menu item usually means the module is not enabled for your tenant or role. A matter may also be absent because it has not been assigned to you.

Ask your firm administrator to review access when:

- a colleague can see a workspace tool that you cannot;
- an expected matter is not listed;
- a feature says that a license or upgrade is required; or
- an integration is disconnected.

Do not share accounts or ask another person to perform work under your identity. Activity, approvals, time entries, and AI actions need a reliable audit trail.

## A useful working rhythm

Start the day with tasks and calendar, do client work from the relevant matter, and record time while the context is fresh. Use communications and document history inside the matter whenever possible so the file tells one coherent story.
