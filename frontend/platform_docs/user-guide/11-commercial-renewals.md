# Commercial contract renewals

The [Contract Renewal Tracker](/plugins/commercial/renewals) helps commercial teams see upcoming renewals before a notice window closes.

## Create a renewal

Enter the contract name, vendor, annual value, business owner, renewal date, notice period, and next action from the executed agreement or another verified source. Use the legal counterparty name when it differs from a product brand.

The renewal date and notice deadline serve different purposes. Confirm whether the agreement uses calendar days or business days, how notice must be delivered, whether auto-renewal applies, and whether amendments changed the original term.

## Operate the tracker

Review upcoming items by urgency. Each item should have a business owner and a concrete next action such as gather usage data, request pricing, send non-renewal notice, or route an amendment for approval.

Link or store the authoritative contract in the appropriate [Matter](/matters). The tracker summarizes obligations; it does not replace the executed agreement.

## Complete or revise

When the parties renew, terminate, or replace an agreement, update the tracker with the verified result and new dates. Preserve the prior decision and source documents according to policy.

Escalate ambiguous notice language, unusually high value, data-security changes, sanctions concerns, or a missed notice window to the responsible attorney or contract owner.
