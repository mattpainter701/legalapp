# Assistant & add-ons

LawHand's assistant can help organize information, search approved sources, and prepare drafts. It is a collaborator inside a controlled workflow, not the final decision-maker.

## Ask better questions

Open the [Assistant](/chat) and state the matter, jurisdiction, task, desired format, and important constraints. Attach or reference the correct source material when the answer should be grounded in a firm document.

Separate facts from assumptions. Ask for uncertainty and missing information to be called out. For research or legal propositions, inspect the cited source and confirm that it supports the specific statement.

## Review every result

Before using output outside LawHand:

- verify names, dates, amounts, quotations, and citations;
- check the governing jurisdiction and current law;
- remove unsupported conclusions;
- confirm privilege, confidentiality, and recipient scope; and
- obtain the approval required by your firm.

An authoritative tone is not evidence of accuracy. If the assistant cannot access a source, it may need you to provide the document or narrow the task.

## Add-on modules

[Add-on Modules](/plugins) contains specialized practice workflows enabled by your firm. A module may have its own portfolio, structured intake, generated profile, or matter-level tools. Follow the same review discipline: structured fields and generated outputs support professional judgment but do not replace it.

If a module says setup is incomplete, contact an administrator. Do not invent firm-wide profile information just to clear a setup gate.

## Report a questionable result

Preserve the prompt, relevant source, output, and matter context. Do not keep retrying with confidential information if you suspect the wrong data scope or an integration problem. Stop and notify your administrator when tenant boundaries, permissions, or source attribution appear incorrect.
