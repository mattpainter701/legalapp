What LawHand does

  • Client and matter CRM — matters, contacts, parties, notes, assignments, budgets, and timelines.
  • Caller intake and Zoom Phone — caller matching, call history, notes, and lead-to-task handoff.
  • Saved conflict reviews — preserved search terms, results, reviewer notes, and decisions.
  • Tasks, deadlines, and calendar — owners, priorities, reminders, and close reasons.
  • Matter communications and email — reviewed forwarding, correspondence history, and email-to-task handoff.
  • Client portal — focused messages, shared documents, dates, signatures, invoices, and balances.
  • Document preparation and automation — DOCX and TXT templates, PDF form filling, review preview, and matter filing.
  • Signature routing and follow-through — sequential signing, delivery state, first-view tracking, reminders, and resend.
  • Time, invoicing, and trust accounting — time and expenses, invoices, payments, retainers, and LEDES export.
  • Source-linked legal research — firm documents and public legal authority with citations for attorney review.
  • Matter-aware AI chat and drafting — every claim labeled cited, verify, or model.
  • Practice-area skill libraries — commercial, litigation, corporate, employment, privacy, IP, real estate, and more.
  • Microsoft 365, Google, and QuickBooks — administrator-enabled connections you can disconnect at any time.
  • MCP for approved AI assistants — tenant-scoped workspace access and a research-only legal authority server, both in gated preview.
  • Tenant isolation and review controls — per-firm isolation, role authorization, and encrypted stored credentials.
