# Corporate-Law AI Assistant Demo Runbook

Audience: corporate and contract attorney with recurring retainer work
Target length: 10 minutes
Tenant: `cybersafeadvisor.com`
Data: synthetic only

## Positioning

Lead with the work outcome:

> LawHand helps you move from client documents to a source-linked issue list,
> reviewed follow-up, and a matter-level work queue. It does not replace your
> judgment, send work without approval, or hide where an answer came from.

Do not lead with model names. If asked, explain that the operator qualifies and
routes paid Standard/Premium capacity while the firm sees understandable service
levels and review controls.

## Preflight — 20 minutes before the meeting

1. Confirm production version/readiness and the active paid demo route.
2. Confirm the three matters and six documents are visible only in the demo tenant.
3. Open each document and verify the synthetic warning is visible.
4. Run each exact prompt below once; keep successful tabs open.
5. Confirm citations open the correct matter document and passage.
6. Confirm task creation opens a review step and does not silently send anything.
7. Confirm the Evergreen Packaging notice and OGC annual review dates.
8. Check the mobile layout and network connection.
9. Keep the source documents and Work Board open as fallback tabs.

If the paid route, citations, tenant isolation, or document access fails, do not
improvise a live production demonstration. Use the prepared artifacts and explain
the expected review path honestly.

## 0:00–1:00 — Set the frame

Show the three matter types:

- customer-side SaaS agreement review;
- Delaware financing and board approvals;
- recurring outside-general-counsel retainer.

Say: “The useful unit is not a clever answer. It is reviewed work attached to the
right matter, source, owner, and deadline.”

## 1:00–4:00 — Contract review with citations

Open **Northstar Analytics - SaaS Vendor Review** and use:

> Review the MSA and Data and Security Addendum from Northstar’s customer-side
> position. Identify the five highest-priority negotiation issues. For each,
> cite the exact source document and clause, explain the business consequence,
> and propose a concrete fallback position. Separate confirmed language from
> questions that require attorney judgment.

Expected anchors:

- perpetual training/product-improvement use of prompts and uploaded documents;
- three-month provider liability cap with uncapped customer obligations;
- no provider indemnity for security/privacy claims;
- 8% renewal uplift and 60-day non-renewal deadline;
- 15-day export window, long backup retention, and no deletion certificate.

Open one citation. Point out that the attorney can inspect the clause before
accepting a finding.

Then ask:

> Prepare reviewed follow-up for the AI data-use restriction and renewal notice.
> Do not send anything. Show the proposed task titles, owners, deadlines, and
> source links first.

If task persistence is not ready, show the prepared Work Board target state and
say that approval/persistence is the next bounded automation—not that it happened.

## 4:00–6:15 — Corporate approval and closing work

Open **HarborLight Robotics - Series Seed Financing** and use:

> Compare the Series Seed term sheet with the board written consent. Identify
> missing approvals, conditions that are not yet satisfied, inconsistencies,
> and items that should return to the board. Build a prioritized closing
> checklist with source citations and a September 15 target close.

Expected anchors:

- stockholder approval and charter filing remain outstanding;
- option-pool math and capitalization certificate need reconciliation;
- invention assignments and key-person arrangements are closing conditions;
- investor counsel fees are capped at $40,000;
- governing documents and director conflicts must be verified;
- material economic changes must return to the board.

Trust line: “The assistant organizes the evidence; counsel still confirms the
charter, bylaws, approvals, conflicts, and final instruments.”

## 6:15–8:30 — Recurring retainer workflow

Open **Redwood Outdoor Supply - Outside General Counsel Retainer** and use:

> Triage the August client requests under the engagement letter. Show the next
> three notice or decision deadlines, included-retainer status, approvals needed,
> and a concise monthly portfolio summary. Cite the engagement and request
> calendar. Recommend tasks, but do not create or send them yet.

Expected anchors:

- 21.4 of 45 included attorney hours used; forecast 43 hours;
- PinePeak exclusivity requires CFO authority and is highest priority;
- Evergreen Packaging non-renewal notice is due September 15;
- warehouse pilot needs data, security, insurance, and conversion review;
- September board materials are due September 22;
- OGC scope/pricing review is July 1, 2027.

This is the retainer payoff: work arrives continuously, but the attorney sees a
matter-aware queue, approaching commitments, and client decisions—not a generic
chat transcript.

## 8:30–10:00 — Mobile follow-through and close

On mobile, show the cited answer and proposed follow-up. Emphasize:

- the source remains attached;
- nothing is sent without approval;
- the same work appears in the matter and Work Board;
- Standard/Premium capacity is operated by LawHand, so the firm does not manage
  provider keys;
- usage and margin controls are part of the platform roadmap, not hidden billing.

Close with:

> The goal is to make recurring legal work easier to review, assign, and finish.
> Which part of your current contract or retainer workflow loses the most time:
> first-pass issue spotting, finding the source again, or getting follow-up done?

## Graceful fallback paths

### Provider is slow or unavailable

Show the prepared source documents and the last successful synthetic response.
State that the paid route is canaried with a rollback revision; do not claim a
live completion succeeded when it did not.

### Retrieval or citation fails

Stop the answer path. Open the source document manually and explain that a
source-unverified answer is intentionally not promoted into matter work.

### Task creation is unavailable

Show the proposed task preview and Work Board. Say “This is awaiting approval”
rather than implying the task was persisted.

### DOCX preview is not page-faithful

Use the original DOCX download and disclose that page-faithful release preview
is tracked as `VA-11`. Do not imply the browser preview is an immutable artifact.

### Authenticated tenant or isolation is uncertain

Do not upload or display the pack. Use local synthetic artifacts only.

## Claims to avoid

- “It gives legal advice” or “replaces review.”
- “Every answer is correct.”
- “Everything is already automated.”
- “All models are private” without provider-specific evidence.
- “The task/email/renewal was created” unless the UI confirms persistence.
- “All cloud work is deployed” when local or unmerged work remains.
