# WellPled image-generation prompts

Mode: built-in image generation with three local visual references. No CLI fallback was used.

Reference roles:

1. Current Clarity social card — composition, typography, warmth, and restraint.
2. Current Clarity editorial illustration — paper collage, source lines, palette, and material language.
3. Earlier WellPled concept board — name and WP-monogram direction only.

## Final identity-board prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final WellPled identity-system presentation.

Create a fully resolved, premium identity board for "WellPled", a modern law-practice operations and source-aware AI platform. The brand meaning is work that is well pleaded and a practice that is well played.

Use precise vector-forward brand design presented with subtle tactile paper texture: modern editorial technology, warm and exact. Inherit Clarity's current warm parchment, midnight navy, muted sage, aged brass, high-contrast editorial serif, neutral sans serif, thin technical rules, spacious grid, calm authority, and restrained depth.

Replace the old appended checkmark with an ownable "WP pleat" symbol. Construct a highly legible W whose center/right fold rises into a clear P bowl; the geometry should feel like briefs, pages, and arguments brought into alignment. Strong silhouette at favicon size. Elegant, not heraldic.

Palette: midnight ink `#14253B`, parchment `#F7F3EC`, warm white `#FFFDF9`, sage `#5A7A5C`, brass `#B8965A`, soft line `#E1D9C9`. No burgundy as a primary color.

Use a wide 16:9 premium brand board with one horizontal lockup, one standalone mark, light/dark use, favicon, product header, and a refined marketing header. Generous negative space; practical applications; no decorative clutter.

Text verbatim: "WellPled" and "Practice, well played." Render each phrase exactly and only as supplied, with no extra body copy or labels.

Avoid gavels, scales, columns, shields, crests, quills, courthouse imagery, old-parchment cosplay, generic checkmarks, generic task-app visuals, burgundy-dominant palette, gradients, 3D logo mockups, stock-logo appearance, and watermarks.

## Final editorial-header prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide WellPled landing-page and campaign header artwork.

Create an elegant abstract editorial scene about complex legal work being brought into order. Multiple translucent briefs, clipped pages, marginal notes, source cards, and fine evidence lines converge into one beautifully aligned pleading at the center-right. A subtle folded W/P rhythm may appear only through the composition of pages and rules, never as a literal logo.

Use a sophisticated mixed-media paper collage with refined product-design geometry, matching Clarity's existing visual world: tactile but clean, premium B2B legal technology.

Use a wide 16:9 composition with a large calm negative-space area on the left for real HTML headline and CTA; visual activity concentrated center-right; readable at website-hero crop and social-header crop. Warm studio daylight, quiet confidence, exactness, preparation, and motion resolved into calm.

Palette: warm parchment `#F7F3EC`, midnight ink `#14253B`, muted sage `#5A7A5C`, aged brass `#B8965A`, and warm white; restrained contrast.

No text, logo, letters, people, or watermark. Avoid gavels, scales, columns, courthouse imagery, law books as cliché, seals, shields, dramatic courtroom lighting, neon, burgundy dominance, blue-tech gradients, generic AI particles, and clutter.
