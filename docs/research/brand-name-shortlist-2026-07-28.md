## Scope and method

Six candidate batches have now been generated across two sessions. Batches 1–3 came
from the prior session; batches 4–6 from this one. Every candidate below was screened
for:

- Legal-tech and software conflicts (web + indexed trademark search)
- Exact-name occupancy in the legal services market
- `.com` registration signal

### Method limitation — read this before trusting any domain claim

The prior session had live Verisign RDAP access. **This session does not** — the
sandbox network policy returns 403 on `rdap.verisign.com`, `rdap.org`, and whois.
All domain data in this document is **DNS-only**: "no A record" means the domain
returned no address record, which is a *candidate signal*, not proof of availability.

DNS calibrated correctly against the prior session's RDAP on three known cases
(`matterahead.com`, `firmahead.com`, `cadenvia.com` — all no A record, all reported
unregistered by RDAP), and it caught real negatives (`matterkeep.com`,
`matterhero.com`, `casehero.com` all resolve). Treat every entry as **verify at
registrar checkout**.

Nothing in this document is a clearance opinion. USPTO guidance requires examining
confusingly similar — not merely identical — marks, related services, state records,
common-law use, and international sources.

## Headliner: MatterWright

> **MatterWright**
> *Every matter, done right.*
> Legal operations for busy firms.

### Rationale

A *wright* is a skilled maker — playwright, shipwright, wheelwright, millwright. It is
a trade-guild suffix meaning someone who builds things properly. It is also a
homophone for *right*. The name therefore reads two ways simultaneously: *the one who
makes your matters*, and *every matter, done right*.

That second reading states the product positioning directly. This is the only
candidate across six batches where the name argues the core claim ("grounded in truth
and accuracy") rather than sitting adjacent to it — and it does so as a quip, which
matches the stated audience brief.

*Wright* is also a top-30 English surname, so the name reads as an institutional firm
name to anyone who does not catch the wordplay. Dual-read: joke for the sharp ones,
brass plaque for everyone else. Same mechanic as Smokeball.

### Identity

**Logo:** a carpenter's square. Simple L-form, single colour, legible at 16px. Means
*true* / *square* / *right angle* — the pun resolves a third time. Resolves the
"nothing to draw" failure that disqualified Clarity, Hadley, and other abstractions.

**Product architecture:** MatterWright Intake · Tasks · Matters · Documents · Billing ·
Research

### Known risks

- **Homophone friction.** Callers will type `matterright.com`. Buying that spelling is
  part of the cost, not optional.
- **Surname read.** Some prospects will assume a law firm until the descriptor lands.
- **No market history.** Zero prior testing of this word on attorneys.

## Three findings that override individual scores

### Finding 1 — Remove the coined masterbrand family on strategy, not clearance

Cadenvia, Ordenvia, Ordevanta, Navendro, Cordenara, and Jurentra all screen clean and
have apparently unregistered `.com`s. They should still be dropped.

The stated audience brief is that attorneys are an established, plain-spoken
professional group who respond to wordplay and institutional credibility. A Latinate
invented word is the single register that reads as venture-backed software marketing to
that buyer. These six are strategically misaligned with the described customer
regardless of how clean the clearance path is.

### Finding 2 — `Counsel___` is riskier than batch 2 scored it

LexisNexis owns **CounselLink+**, a major matter-management product
(`lexisnexis.com/en-us/products/counsellink/matter-management.page`). A well-resourced
incumbent holding `Counsel` + word in the same product category makes CounselNorth,
CounselSpan, CounselAxis, CounselAhead, CounselHelm, and CounselWright materially worse
bets than their original scores indicate.

Additionally, **FIRMLOGIC** is registered to Womble Bond Dickinson (US) LLP for
business management consultation for law firms — an AmLaw firm actively holds a
`Firm` + word mark in an adjacent service.

### Finding 3 — The `Matter___` prefix has live marks in the exact goods

| Mark | Owner | Goods |
|---|---|---|
| **MATTERO** | Lawmaster Pty Ltd | Legal practice management software — matter management, accounting, document management |
| **MATTERTRACK** | — | Software for management of outside legal counsel |
| (24 marks) | Matterfi Inc. | Computer/software class |

Neither blocks outright. But an examiner has live hooks against **any** `Matter` +
word application. **Counsel should price clearance for the prefix family, not
name-by-name.** This applies equally to MatterWright, MatterMarshal, MatterStead,
MatterArc, and MatterAhead.

### Also still true — Clarity Legal must be retired

Clarity Legal Software LLC has common-law priority from approximately 2007 in the exact
goods (their federal application, serial 77159846, went abandoned in 2009 — which ends
the registration, not the rights). At least five further legal entities use the wording,
including Clarity Legal Group® (NC), Clarity Law Firm (MI), Clarity Law (NC), Legal
Clarity Group (NY), and Clarity Ltd (IT services to the legal sector). "Legal" would be
disclaimed on any application, leaving only the laudatory term "Clarity" as the
protectable element.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Registrar checkout** — `matterwright.com`, `matterright.com`, `matterwrite.com`;
   MatterMarshal cluster as hedge. Approx. $50. Registration preserves options; it is
   not a commitment to the brand.
2. **Blind attorney/staff test** — MatterWright vs MatterMarshal vs MatterArc vs
   FirmThread. Predicted outcome: MatterWright wins wordplay and audience fit;
   MatterMarshal wins visual identity; MatterArc wins immediate comprehension.
3. **Trademark counsel** — comprehensive clearance on the winner **plus the `Matter`
   prefix family**, Classes 9 and 42.
4. **Rename** — only after steps 1–3.

### Rename scope (measured 2026-07-28)

- 175 files contain the string; ~766 occurrences
- Mechanical for the large majority (marketing copy, email templates, PDF metadata,
  API title, manifest)
- **Two require migration decisions, not find-and-replace:**
  - `claritylegal-records` — the root folder created in customer OneDrive/Google Drive
    (`backend/app/services/cloud_init.py`, 24 code sites). Renaming affects live
    customer cloud storage.
  - `clarity-legal` — MCP `serverInfo.name` and `PLATFORM_TOKEN_ISSUER`
    (`backend/app/services/mcp_protocol.py`, `platform_auth.py`, `routers/mcp.py`).
    Changing the token issuer invalidates existing platform tokens.
- `clarity_app` (PostgreSQL role) can remain — internal, not customer-visible.
